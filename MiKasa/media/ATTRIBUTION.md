# Retro Cinema clips — origins and reuse

These four silent H.264 MP4s are transcoded from archival drive-in footage
hosted by Wikimedia Commons. The MP4 versions and poster JPEGs are included
locally so the Mi Kasa wallpaper works offline (no streaming service required).

| Bundled video | Original | Licensing recorded on Commons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `drivein-hotdog-1.mp4` | [Drive-in movie hot dog ad 1.ogv](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drive-in_movie_hot_dog_ad_1.ogv) | US public domain, no copyright renewal |
| `drivein-hotdog-2.mp4` | [Drive-in movie hot dog ad 2.ogv](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drive-in_movie_hot_dog_ad_2.ogv) | US public domain, no copyright renewal |
| `drivein-snackbar.mp4` | [Snackbar animation1.webm](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Snackbar_animation1.webm) | CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication |
| `drivein-lobby-hd.mp4` | [Let's All go to the Lobby!.webm](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Let%27s_All_go_to_the_Lobby!.webm) | US public domain, no copyright renewal. Transcoded from the 4K restoration to 1080p H.264. |

The Commons descriptions include source links back to archive.org. Public-domain
status for the vintage films is identified for the United States; copyright
terms can differ in other jurisdictions. Audio was deliberately removed so
clips can autoplay muted behind Mi Kasa.

This is archival advertising footage, not advertising served to you by a third
party. Mi Kasa does not track viewing or load remote advertising during playback.
